A modern, responsive, corporate one-page portfolio website developed for **Syed Shujat Shah**, an authorized insurance consultant representing **State Life Insurance Corporation of Pakistan**.

The platform is designed to provide clients, expatriates, and families with clear, transparent information on both **Conventional Life Insurance Plans** and Shariah-compliant **Islamic Takaful** solutions.

## 🎯 Key Features

- **Sticky Navigation & Brand Header**:
  - Official State Life Corporation branding (`statelife.jpeg`).
  - Quick contact indicators (phone, email, working hours).
  - Prominent **"WhatsApp Now"** instant consultation button.
  - Smooth mobile hamburger menu drawer.

- **Hero Showcase**:
  - Catchy value proposition: *"Protect the ones you love with certainty & peace of mind."*
  - Dual call-to-actions (*Explore Plans* and *Get Free Quote*).
  - Floating badges highlighting **Family Protection** and **Smart Savings**.
  - Key trust statistics (15+ Years Experience, 3,500+ Families Insured, 99% Claims Assistance).

- **Why Choose Us (Core Value Pillars)**:
  - 100% Sovereign Guarantee backed by the Government of Pakistan.
  - SECP-compliant Shariah Takaful governance.
  - Annual compound reversionary bonuses.
  - End-to-end personal claims advocacy.

- **Interactive Tabbed Plan Filtering**:
  - Filter effortlessly between **All**, **Conventional**, and **Islamic Takaful** offerings.
  - Smooth scale & fade animations when switching categories.
  - Cards include plan tags, highlights, and an **"Ask about this plan"** trigger that automatically pre-selects the plan in the contact form.

- **Advisor Showcase ("Meet Your Advisor")**:
  - Professional consultant portrait (`Gemini_Generated_Image_ym07l3ym07l3ym07.jpg`).
  - Bio detailing over 15 years of industry excellence.
  - Trust points covering official representation, claim assistance, and custom premium structuring.

- **Two-Column Contact Section**:
  - **Left Panel**: Corporate dark-blue card with direct phone, WhatsApp link, email, and office address.
  - **Right Panel**: Consultation request form with client validation, plan selector, and feedback messages.

- **Floating WhatsApp Quick Button**:
  - Persistent bottom-right floating trigger with hover tooltip for instant messaging.

## 🚀 Getting Started Locally

1. **Clone the repository:**
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/n0OBiL/Insurance-Portfolio.git
   ```

2. **Navigate into the folder:**
   ```bash
   cd Insurance-Portfolio
   ```

3. **Open in your browser:**
   - Double-click `index.html`, or
   - Use VS Code Live Server extension, or run:
     ```bash
     npx serve .
     ```
